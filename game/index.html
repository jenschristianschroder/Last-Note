Field guide

Keeping a note alive

  1. Strike the fork. A two-beat return earns one resonance in the first movement. Your fork fades if three beats pass between echoes.
  2. Place a glass bell. Earn three resonance, then tap a socket. On the long circuit, a bell on beat two divides the journey into two safe gaps. You can move a bell for free.
  3. Record eight stable bars. Each long return completes one bar. Changing the layout starts the count again. If the fork fades, adjust the circuit and strike it again.
  4. Layer the next movement. Recorded phrases earn resonance each bar, including up to eight hours between visits. Stagger a phrase to send a supporting echo, or play in unison to double a long return.
  5. Conduct a rest. From movement three, queue a rest on a long return. Each held beat doubles its value. Release before the fork fades. A mounted bell answers on the third held beat.
